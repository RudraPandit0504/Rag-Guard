On March 3, 2024, the customer authentication service was unavailable for 47 minutes starting at 2:14 AM UTC. The root cause was a certificate on the internal auth gateway that expired without triggering the automated renewal job, which had silently failed two weeks earlier due to a permissions change on the renewal script. The on-call engineer manually issued a new certificate and restarted the gateway pods. Resolution included fixing the renewal job's permissions and adding an alert for certificates nearing expiry within 14 days.

On June 18, 2024, the primary Qdrant vector database cluster experienced a 2 hour and 10 minute outage beginning at 11:40 AM UTC, causing all retrieval-augmented queries to fail. The cause was a disk volume on the leader node filling to capacity after a backup process failed to rotate old snapshots. Engineers freed disk space manually and failed over to a replica while the leader was rebuilt. The team later added automated snapshot rotation with a 7-day retention limit and disk usage alerts at 80 percent capacity.

On August 2, 2024, the billing API returned elevated error rates for 34 minutes starting at 4:05 PM UTC, affecting roughly 12 percent of checkout attempts. Investigation traced the issue to a deployment that introduced a database query missing an index, causing timeouts under peak load. The on-call team rolled back the deployment within 20 minutes of the first alert. The missing index was added in a follow-up migration, and the pipeline was updated to require a query performance check before merges affecting the billing schema.

On October 9, 2024, the support ticketing system was fully unreachable for 1 hour and 5 minutes starting at 9:02 AM UTC, during a period of high ticket volume following a product announcement. The cause was an autoscaling misconfiguration that capped the service at half its normal maximum instance count after a routine infrastructure update reset the limit. Engineers manually raised the instance cap to restore service. The autoscaling configuration was moved into version-controlled infrastructure code to prevent future overrides from being lost.

On January 14, 2025, email notifications for password resets and account alerts were delayed by up to 3 hours starting at 6:50 AM UTC and continuing for approximately 5 hours. The cause was the third-party email provider throttling the account after it exceeded its sending quota, following a spike in alerts from a misfiring monitoring rule. The rule was corrected to stop the alert loop, and the team negotiated a higher sending quota, along with a fallback provider for future throttling events.

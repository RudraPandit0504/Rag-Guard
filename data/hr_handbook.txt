Full-time employees at Northwind Systems accrue 15 days of paid vacation per year during their first two years, increasing to 20 days after the second anniversary and 25 after the fifth. Vacation requests should be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for single days and 3 weeks in advance for absences longer than a week. Sick leave is separate and totals 10 days per year, with unused days carrying over up to a maximum of 15 accumulated. Bereavement leave provides up to 5 paid days for immediate family and 2 for extended family.

Standard working hours are 9 AM to 6 PM local time, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Core hours, during which all employees must be reachable, run from 10 AM to 4 PM; employees may shift start and end times outside that window with manager approval. Overtime for non-exempt hourly staff is paid at 1.5 times the base rate for hours beyond 40 in a week and requires prior manager sign-off in the timesheet system.

Remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week for roles that do not require physical presence, subject to manager approval renewed each quarter. Fully remote arrangements are considered case-by-case by the department head and HR business partner. Remote employees must maintain the same core-hours availability and attend any meeting marked mandatory regardless of location. Equipment, including a laptop and monitor, is provided by IT on request, with a one-time home office stipend of 300 dollars in the first year.

Performance reviews occur twice a year, in April and October. Each cycle includes a self-assessment, a manager evaluation, and for senior employees and above, peer feedback from two to three colleagues. Ratings use a five-point scale, and any rating below the midpoint triggers a mandatory improvement conversation within 2 weeks of the review being finalized. Compensation adjustments and promotions are considered primarily during October, aligned with the annual budget process.

The code of conduct requires employees to disclose conflicts of interest, including outside employment and financial stakes in vendors or competitors, through the annual disclosure form. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited and can be reported confidentially through the ethics hotline or directly to HR. Confidential information, including unreleased product plans and customer data, may not be shared outside the company. Violations are investigated by HR and can result in disciplinary action up to termination, depending on severity.

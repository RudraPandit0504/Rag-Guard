Northwind Systems accepts returns on physical hardware within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is in its original packaging with all accessories. Refunds go to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after inspection. Software licenses and digital subscriptions are non-refundable once activated. Customers must open a case through the support portal and receive a Return Merchandise Authorization number before shipping anything back; packages without one are refused at the dock. A 15 percent restocking fee applies to opened peripherals, unless the return is due to a confirmed defect.

Customer data is classified into three tiers: public, internal, and restricted. Billing information, government identifiers, and authentication credentials fall under the restricted tier and may only be accessed by the Payments and Identity teams. Exporting restricted data to a spreadsheet or third-party tool requires written approval from a data protection officer, logged in the access ledger maintained by Security. Customer records are retained for 24 months after account closure, then purged during the quarterly deletion cycle. Employees who suspect a data handling violation should file a report within 48 hours.

Employee accounts require a password of at least 14 characters, including one number and one symbol, rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for the customer database, deployment pipeline, and finance system; SMS codes are not accepted, only authenticator apps or hardware keys. Shared logins are prohibited; service accounts instead use API tokens with a maximum lifetime of 90 days. Requests for elevated access go through the IT Access Committee, which meets every Tuesday and typically responds within 3 business days. Access for former employees is revoked within 4 hours of an offboarding record being filed with HR.

Support tickets are triaged into four severity levels. A Severity 1 incident, a full outage affecting all customers, must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and escalated to the engineering duty manager if unresolved after 1 hour. Severity 2 issues, such as degraded performance for a subset of accounts, carry a 1-hour acknowledgment window and a 4-hour escalation threshold. Severity 3 and 4 tickets are handled during business hours with no mandatory escalation timer. A ticket untouched for 24 hours is flagged to the support team lead; further escalations reach the VP of Engineering, paged directly for payment-processing incidents.

Employees may submit expense reimbursements for approved travel, client meals, and conference registration. Requests must be filed within 60 days of the expense date and include an itemized receipt for any charge over 25 dollars. Meals during travel are capped at 75 dollars per day, and alcohol is not reimbursable. Airfare must be booked in economy class unless the flight exceeds 6 hours, in which case premium economy is permitted with manager approval. Reimbursements process on a biweekly cycle, with funds arriving within 10 business days of approval.
